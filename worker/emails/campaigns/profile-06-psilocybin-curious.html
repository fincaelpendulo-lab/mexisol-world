PSILOCYBIN AT THE FINCA

A softer way in

Reconnect with your body, your intuition and the women around you.

Psilocybin has a gentle, earthy way of opening the heart. Many women tell us it helped them hear their own voice again.

Our psilocybin retreats are led by Krista, with a small group, a calm rhythm and time in nature between ceremonies. Three ways to stay connected until then:

Listen to your body

Before deciding, pause and ask where you feel it. Your body often knows first.

Gather with women

A circle, a walk, a shared meal. We heal faster in good company.

Create a small altar

Flowers, a candle, something from nature. A place to return to yourself each day.
