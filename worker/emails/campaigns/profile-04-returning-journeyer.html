What is new at the Finca for 2027.

You have sat in the circle with us. You know a retreat is not the end of the work but a doorway into it.

Keeping the thread alive between journeys is what turns insight into change:

Return to your intention

Read what you wrote before your last ceremony. What has shifted? What is still asking for attention?

Keep a daily practice

Meditation, breath, movement or prayer. Ten minutes held every day goes further than an hour now and then.

Share what you learned

Tell one person what the medicine showed you. Saying it out loud helps it land.
