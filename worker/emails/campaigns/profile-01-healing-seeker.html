A NOTE FOR YOU

Healing is not a straight line

Some days feel lighter, some feel heavy. Both are part of it.

Since you were with us, life has kept moving. Maybe some of what opened at the Finca is still with you. Maybe some of it faded, and that is normal.

Change rarely happens in one week. It grows from small moments of attention, repeated. Here are three that our guests come back to.

Start the day outside

Five minutes of sun and air before your phone. Notice your feet on the ground and three things you can hear.

Write one honest line

Each night, one sentence about how you really feel. No fixing, just naming it.

Let someone in

Reach out to one person this week. Connection is part of the medicine.
