Choose how you close the year

Lodging and activities are included in every package. Prices are per person in Mexican pesos and depend on your room.

New Year Retreat
December 28 to January 2 · 5 nights · from $9,350 MXN per person

December 31 to January 2 · 2 nights · from $4,750 MXN per person
An ayahuasca ceremony, New Year dinner with a bonfire and song, and a slow first day of the year with myths and mantras.

December 31 to January 1 · 1 night · from $1,650 MXN per person
New Year dinner, a bonfire celebration and a concert. No ceremony.
