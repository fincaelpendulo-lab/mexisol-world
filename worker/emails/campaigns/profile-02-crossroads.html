AS THE YEAR TURNS

When one chapter closes

Endings make room for what comes next.

Many of our guests arrive at a turning point. A relationship ends, work changes, someone is gone, or life simply asks for something new.

The end of the year is a good moment to pause and listen. Three questions to take with you on a quiet walk:

What am I ready to leave behind?

Not what you should drop. What you are ready to.

What gave me energy this year?

Small things count. Follow them.

What would I do if I trusted myself?

Write the first answer that comes, before the doubts arrive.
