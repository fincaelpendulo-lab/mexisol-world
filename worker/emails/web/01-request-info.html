WHO WE ARE

MexiSol World grew around Finca El Péndulo, 14 acres of countryside in Tepatitlán, Jalisco, about an hour from Guadalajara airport. Since 2017 more than 500 people have sat in ceremony with us. Humberto leads our ayahuasca ceremonies, Krista our psilocybin work, and Marina looks after the land and everyone on it. Groups are small, up to 15 guests.
