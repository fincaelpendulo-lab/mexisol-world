THE WEEK

The schedule moves with the group, and every ceremony is optional. A typical ayahuasca week looks like this:

DAY 1Check in, walk the land, meet the group over dinner.
DAY 2Intentions, a quiet afternoon, first ceremony at night.
DAY 3Rest, journaling and a Satori ceremony.
DAY 4Optional yoga or breathwork, one-on-one time, second ceremony.
DAY 5Group integration and a fire circle at night.
DAY 6Sunrise hike, temazcal, cacao in the Osho Hut, closing dinner.
DAY 7Breakfast, goodbyes, ride to the airport.
