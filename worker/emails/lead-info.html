Who we are

MexiSol World grew around Finca El Péndulo, 14 acres of countryside in Tepatitlán, Jalisco, about an hour from Guadalajara airport. Since 2017 more than 500 people have come to sit in ceremony with us. Humberto leads our ayahuasca ceremonies, Krista our psilocybin work, and Marina looks after the land and everyone on it. Groups are small, up to 15 guests. We do not promise miracles: we offer a safe room, people who know what they are doing, and medicines used with care for generations.
