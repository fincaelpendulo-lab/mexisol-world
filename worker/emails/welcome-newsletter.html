A LETTER TO OUR FAMILY

Dear Family,

Some of you have shared sacred ceremonies with us. Others have experienced the magic of Finca El Péndulo, the music, the laughter, the connections, and the unforgettable moments that bring us together.

And for all of those shared experiences, we are deeply grateful.

Today, we are excited to introduce you to MexiSol World.

A new and independent project bringing together Humberto Alcalá, Krista, Marina, and our beautiful team at Finca El Péndulo.

OUR VISION

Rooted in Mexico, reaching far beyond

MexiSol World brings together sacred retreats, plant medicine ceremonies, international journeys, music, festivals, and gatherings that celebrate life, connection, and transformation.

And we would love for you to be part of this new adventure.
