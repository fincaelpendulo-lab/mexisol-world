## Sources (all opened and read)

### Talks and interviews
3. **The Tim Ferriss Show #298: Dr. Gabor Maté, New Paradigms, Ayahuasca, and Redefining Addiction**, 2018.
   Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H9B5mYfBPlY Transcript: https://tim.blog/2018/06/04/the-tim-ferriss-show-transcripts-dr-gabor-mate/ (interview)
   Maté is a physician and author who has drunk and studied ayahuasca. Ferriss: "you set a clear intention, but the clear
   intention is not the same as an expectation." Maté: "the specific intention is what do I want to learn tonight";
   "some people sit through the whole night resisting their experience because it doesn't meet their expectations";
   "it's not that the ayahuasca comes with an agenda. It works through you"; "whatever experience you have, that's the experience you need to have."
4. **The Tim Ferriss Show #523: Dennis McKenna, The Depths of Ayahuasca**, 2021. https://tim.blog/2021/07/25/dennis-mckenna-transcript/ (interview)
   Ethnopharmacologist with 500+ sessions. "the message is not the same every time. There is no standard message."
   Agrees "set and setting is so important." Advises "Keep listening."

### Research
9. **Haijen et al. (2018) Predicting Responses to Psychedelics: A Prospective Study**, Frontiers in Pharmacology. https://doi.org/10.3389/fphar.2018.00897 (prospective observational survey)
   654 people planning a psychedelic experience on their own, surveyed before and up to 4 weeks after (212 at 4 weeks).
   "Having 'clear intentions' for the experience was conducive to mystical-type experiences." A "set" of feeling
   prepared, open and ready to surrender, with low anxiety, predicted fewer challenging experiences. Self-selected, uncontrolled.
10. **Bathje, Fenton, Pillersdorf and Hill (2021) A Qualitative Study of Intention and Impact of Ayahuasca Use by Westerners**, Journal of Humanistic Psychology. https://doi.org/10.1177/00221678211008331 (qualitative interviews)
   41 Western participants in facilitated group ayahuasca, interviewed about intentions and perceived lasting impact.
   Benefits in areas often focused on in therapy and "extratherapeutic" areas: creativity, physical sensations, connection to
   nature, spirituality, concern for the greater good. Two reported problematic experiences, apparently related to set and setting.
11. **Breau, J. (2025) With Best Intention: Psychedelic Intention-Setting Rituals as Rites of Sacralization**, Harvard Study of Psychedelics in Society and Culture / CSWR. https://psychedelics-study.harvard.edu/?p=2067 (academic essay, abstract read)
   Ethnographic research at Burning Man; argues intention-setting rituals mark a psychedelic experience "as special and sacred."
