## Sources

### Academic and government health

1. **Adverse effects of ayahuasca: Results from the Global Ayahuasca Survey.** Bouso JC, Andión Ó, Sarris JJ,
   Scheidegger M, Tófoli LF, Opaleye ES, Schubert V, Perkins D. PLOS Global Public Health, 16 Nov 2022.
   https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgph.0000438. Type: cross-sectional online survey (non-random), n = 10,836.
   69.9% reported at least one acute physical effect; vomiting/nausea 62.0%; 2.3% needed medical attention.
   55.9% reported a mental effect in the weeks/months after; 12% sought professional support; about 88% saw the
   effects as part of a positive growth process. Religious settings linked to fewer adverse effects; adverse
   physical effects linked to existing physical conditions.
2. **Ayahuasca Tourism: Participants in Shamanic Rituals and their Personality Styles, Motivation, Benefits and
   Risks.** Kavenská V, Simonová H. Journal of Psychoactive Drugs 47(5):351-9, 2015.
   https://doi.org/10.1080/02791072.2015.1094590 (PMID 26514589). Type: questionnaire and interview study, N = 77
   people who travelled to South America for ayahuasca. Risks they named: lack of trust in the shaman or
   organizer, inaccurate information from the shaman or organizer, exposure to dangerous situations.
3. **Drug tourism or spiritual healing? Ayahuasca seekers in Amazonia.** Winkelman M. Journal of Psychoactive
   Drugs 37(2):209-18, 2005. https://doi.org/10.1080/02791072.2005.10399803 (PMID 16149335). Type: interview study
   at one Amazonian retreat (classic). Main motives: spiritual development, emotional healing, self-awareness;
   argues against the "drug tourist" label.
4. **CDC Travelers' Health: Peru.** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Reviewed 1 Oct 2026.
   https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/destinations/traveler/none/peru. Yellow fever vaccine recommended for travelers
   ≥9 months going to areas <2,300 m in listed regions including Loreto (updated 12 Feb 2026). Malaria in all
   areas <2,500 m east of the Andes "including the cities of Iquitos and Puerto Maldonado"; prophylaxis
   recommended (updated 23 Apr 2025).
5. **CDC Travelers' Health: Mexico.** CDC. Reviewed 3 Sep 2026.
   https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/destinations/traveler/none/mexico. Malaria in Campeche, Chiapas and southern
   Chihuahua; rare in Oaxaca, Sinaloa, Sonora, Tabasco. Jalisco not listed. Yellow fever vaccine not recommended.
6. **Foreign travel advice: Peru, safety and security.** UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office.
   https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/peru/safety-and-security. "Spiritual cleansing" (ayahuasca or San
   Pedro) offered in the Amazon area, northern Peru and Cusco; brew "not regulated"; people have become seriously
   ill and some died; "retreats are usually far from populated areas, making it difficult to get medical
   attention"; reports of sexual assault.
7. **Foreign travel advice: Mexico.** UK FCDO, updated 20 Oct 2026. https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/mexico.
   In Jalisco advises against all but essential travel to areas south and south-west of Lake Chapala to the
   Colima border and to listed northern municipalities. Guadalajara, Tepatitlán and Los Altos not named.
8. **Declaran Patrimonio Cultural de la Nación a los conocimientos y usos tradicionales del ayahuasca.** Andina
   (Peru's state news agency), 12 Jul 2008.
   https://andina.pe/ingles/noticia-declaran-patrimonio-cultural-de-nacion-a-los-conocimientos-y-usos-tradicionales-del-ayahuasca-184254.aspx.
   Instituto Nacional de Cultura, Resolución Directoral Nacional N° 836/INC, declared the traditional knowledge
   and uses of ayahuasca practised by native Amazonian communities national cultural heritage, to protect
   traditional use and the sacred character of the ritual, as distinct from decontextualised Western,
   commercial use.
9. **Loreto (official destination page).** PromPerú, peru.travel. https://www.peru.travel/en/destinations/loreto.
   Lima to Iquitos by air about 1 h 40 min; connections via Tarapoto or Pucallpa.
10. **Largest city with no major road connection.** Guinness World Records.
    https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/101153-largest-city-with-no-major-road-connection. Iquitos,
    population around 430,000; only road stops at Nauta about 100 km south; "accessible only by air and river".

### News and long-form media

11. **La fiscalía investiga la muerte de un estadounidense tras consumir ayahuasca en la Amazonía peruana.** AFP,
    published by Swissinfo, 6 Jun 2025.
    https://www.swissinfo.ch/spa/la-fiscal%C3%ADa-investiga-la-muerte-de-un-estadounidense-tras-consumir-ayahuasca-en-la-amazon%C3%ADa-peruana/89474970.
    Lodge in a native community about five hours by river from Iquitos; prosecutors examining whether organizers
    were qualified; ayahuasca spiritual tourism has grown in Loreto, Ucayali and San Martín for more than two
    decades.
12. **Loreto: ciudadano norteamericano muere tras sesión de ayahuasca.** Redacción El Comercio (Peru), 3 Jun 2025.
    https://elcomercio.pe/peru/loreto/loreto-norteamericano-muere-tras-sesion-de-ayahuasca-ultimas-noticia/.
    Reports he had not disclosed he was taking antibiotics, which "could have caused a serious adverse
    reaction"; notes the 2008 heritage declaration.
13. **Tourism Opens New Doors, Creates New Challenges, for Traditional Healers in Peru.** Rachel Proctor. Cultural
    Survival Quarterly, 2 Apr 2010.
    https://www.culturalsurvival.org/publications/cultural-survival-quarterly/tourism-opens-new-doors-creates-new-challenges-traditional.
    Shipibo healers in San Francisco de Yarinacocha (near Pucallpa) from families of curanderos; training through
    long periods drinking ayahuasca and fasting; tourism brings income and also pressure on tradition.

### Creators and YouTube

14. **Should Ayahuasca Tourism in Peru Be Regulated?** Emily Sinclair (PhD candidate, anthropologist).
    Chacruna Institute, 8 Sep 2018. https://chacruna.net/should-ayahuasca-tourism-peru-be-regulated/. Around 150
    ayahuasca centres and suppliers in the Iquitos region, most unregistered; "anyone can set up an ayahuasca
    center"; main risks sexual abuse, psychological breakdowns, health complications with heart problems or
    medications such as antidepressants; fatalities "extremely rare".
15. **Keynote Talk: Evgenia Fotiou, Integration in Ayahuasca or Shamanic Tourism (14 Jun 2024).** Exeter
    Psychedelics Group (University of Exeter conference channel), YouTube, uploaded 10 Oct 2024, 57 min.
    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Px88jbsMd4. Fotiou (University of Crete) did 18 months of fieldwork in
    Iquitos for her PhD. Abstract: westerners seek healing in ayahuasca rituals "now offered in numerous retreats
    in Peru and beyond"; how rituals are framed by facilitators helps participants integrate. Credible: academic
    anthropologist, university conference.
