# Research: retreat-for-nurses-burnout

Profile: Caregiver. Keyword: "retreat for nurses burnout" / "retiro para personal de salud con burnout".
Searcher's question: "I give care all day and I'm empty. What would it mean to be cared for for a week?"
All sources opened and read on 2026-11-10.

## Books

1. **Trauma Stewardship: An Everyday Guide to Caring for Self While Caring for Others**. Laura van Dernoot Lipsky
   with Connie Burk. Berrett-Koehler, 2009. https://traumastewardship.com/trauma-stewardship-book/ (book)
   - Author's page: the book "is for anyone who's trying to make the world a better place while reckoning with the
     personal toll that effort can take". Contains "16 warning signs that you may be absorbing more than you realize".
   - Publisher and year (Berrett-Koehler, 2009) confirmed in library catalogue records found in search.
   - Lipsky founded the Trauma Stewardship Institute and has worked nearly 40 years with many fields.

2. **Kitchen Table Wisdom: Stories That Heal**. Rachel Naomi Remen, M.D. Riverhead, 1996 (10th anniversary
   edition 2006). https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/348294/kitchen-table-wisdom-by-rachel-naomi-remen/ (book)
   - Publisher page: Remen counseled people with chronic and terminal illness for more than twenty years; cofounder
     and medical director of the Commonweal Cancer Help Program; clinical professor at UCSF School of Medicine.
   - The book is a collection of true stories; "We may need to listen to one another's stories again."

3. **How Can I Help? Stories and Reflections on Service**. Ram Dass and Paul Gorman. Knopf, 1985.
   https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/a/ram-dass-paul-gorman/how-can-i-help1/ (book, via Kirkus review)
   - Kirkus: a philosophy of service "particularly slanted toward social workers, nurses, doctors, and other
     therapy-providers"; argues self-work supports helping and helping supports the helper; first-person caregiver stories.
   - Kirkus is critical: finds it leaves too little room for tragedy and futility.

4. (ES only) **Sé amable contigo mismo: El arte de la compasión hacia uno mismo**. Kristin Neff. Paidós, 2024 edition
   (Spanish translation of Self-Compassion, 2011). https://www.planetadelibros.com/libro-se-amable-contigo-mismo/400342 (book)
   - Paidós longseller relaunched 2024; synopsis opens: "La incansable búsqueda de una autoestima sana se ha
     convertido en una religión tiránica." Neff is a pioneer researcher in self-compassion (UT Austin).

## Talks

5. **Beyond the Cliff**. Laura van Dernoot Lipsky, TEDxWashingtonCorrectionsCenterforWomen. TEDx Talks, YouTube,
   uploaded 2015-04-23. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uOzDGrcvmus (talk; transcript read)
   - Credible: founder of Trauma Stewardship Institute; worked years at Harborview, the level one trauma center for the Northwest.
   - Cliff story: on a family hike in the Caribbean she wondered where the helicopter would land; her stepfather-in-law
     asked "Are you sure all this trauma work hasn't gotten to you?" The toll is "slow moving", hard to gauge, and isolating.
   - Signs she lists: feeling you are never doing enough, hypervigilance, exhaustion ("tired in your soul"), avoidance,
     cynical humor, anger, addictions, numbing. Calls it vicarious trauma, compassion fatigue, secondary trauma.
   - "you don't get to selectively numb": numbing sorrow also numbs happiness. Quotes Howard Thurman: "what the world
     needs is people who have come alive." Offers presence as the practice.

6. **The Space Between Self-Esteem and Self Compassion**. Kristin Neff, TEDxCentennialParkWomen. TEDx Talks,
   YouTube, 2013. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IvtZBUSplr4 (talk; transcript read)
   - Neff: researcher who spent ten years studying self-compassion.
   - Three components: kindness vs harsh self-judgment, common humanity, mindfulness.
   - "We say things to ourselves we would never say to someone we cared about."
   - "the more we were able to keep our hearts open to ourselves, the more we have available to give to others."
     "You just have to remember to be a good friend to yourself."

## Media

9. **Psilocybin Helped Doctors and Nurses Who Worked Through the Pandemic**. Alexander Lekhtman, Filter, 2024-12-10.
   https://filtermag.org/psilocybin-doctors-nurses-covid/ (media)
   - Participant quotes from the trial: "I don't have to carry it all by myself." "I was shown my tendency to be a superhero".
   - Back: burnout is emotional exhaustion, cynicism and low personal accomplishment; "It starts at work, but over time
     it bleeds into your whole life".
   - Limits: small sample (over 2,200 applied), unblinding, placebo group offered psilocybin after day 28; one nausea
     case needing attention; one participant said weeks later they would be "better off dead" but showed no suicidal ideation.

10. **Helping, Fixing or Serving?** Rachel Naomi Remen, Lion's Roar, 2021-10-25. https://www.lionsroar.com/helping-fixing-or-serving/ (essay)
    - "When you help, you see life as weak. When you fix, you see life as broken. When you serve, you see life as whole."
    - "Fixing and helping are draining, and over time we may burn out, but service is renewing."
    - Service is "a relationship between equals".

## Research

11. **Back AL et al. (2024). Psilocybin Therapy for Clinicians With Symptoms of Depression From Frontline Care During
    the COVID-19 Pandemic: A Randomized Clinical Trial.** JAMA Network Open 7(12):e2449026.
    https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2024.49026 (PMID 39636638) (RCT)
    - 30 physicians, APPs and nurses (15 psilocybin 25 mg, 15 niacin), no pre-pandemic diagnosis, moderate/severe depression.
    - Each: 2 preparation visits, 1 medication session, 3 integration visits.
    - MADRS change day 28: -21.33 vs -9.33, difference -12.00, P < .001. Burnout (SPFI): -6.40 vs -2.33, P = .05,
      not statistically significant. PTSD only descriptive. Two errata (2025).

12. **Andrews H, Tierney S, Seers K (2020). Needing permission: The experience of self-care and self-compassion in
    nursing: A constructivist grounded theory study.** International Journal of Nursing Studies 101:103436.
    https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijnurstu.2019.103436 (qualitative)
    - 30 NHS nurses interviewed 2015 to 2016. Themes: nursing as calling vs job; need for a stable base; managing
      emotions of caring. Core: nurses need permission, from others and themselves, to be self-caring and self-compassionate.
    - Nurses often struggle most with self-compassion.
