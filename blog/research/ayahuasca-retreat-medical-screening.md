# Research: ayahuasca-retreat-medical-screening

Profile: Healing Seeker. Keyword: "ayahuasca retreat medical screening" / "evaluación médica para retiro de ayahuasca".
Searcher question: What will they ask me before I can join, and why does it matter beyond the medical part?

## House facts and site (MexiSol)

- blog/house-facts.md (2026-11-06): screening has three parts: (1) medications and the taper, always with the
  prescribing doctor; (2) an evaluation of why the guest is coming; (3) their commitment to the process of
  transformation. No other steps unless the medical page states them. No good or bad experiences. Guests can choose a
  smaller dose and ask for more. Full retreat week only. Integration coach is a woman.
- src/pages/medical.astro: "Nothing is confirmed until we review your health". At registration we ask about medical
  history, every medication, supplement and herb, and any recent use of other substances; we go over it on a call.
  Some conditions mean the retreat is not safe; others need clearance from the guest's own doctor or a change in which
  medicines they take part in. Never stop a prescribed medication on your own. Lists conditions (epilepsy, psychosis or
  bipolar, serious heart disease, active dependence on alcohol, benzodiazepines or opioids, pregnancy, etc.).
- src/components/pages/BookCall.astro: on the call "we talk about what brings you, your health and medications, and
  which retreat and date make sense for you". "Nothing is confirmed or charged until then." Have your list of
  medications and supplements at hand.
- Existing post ayahuasca-and-ssris: "Before you arrive, we confirm that the plan was followed and that you are safe
  to take part."
- Existing posts ayahuasca-retreat-cost / after-ayahuasca-integration: group and one-on-one integration during the
  week, and "we stay in touch after you go home". Seven days, three ayahuasca ceremonies.

### Research

10. Haijen, E. C. H. M. et al. (2018). Predicting Responses to Psychedelics: A Prospective Study. Frontiers in
    Pharmacology 9:897. https://doi.org/10.3389/fphar.2018.00897 (prospective observational online survey)
    - 654 people planning to take a psychedelic at baseline, 212 at four weeks. A "set" factor (comfort, openness,
      preparedness, mood, readiness to surrender) was associated with less challenging experiences. "Clear intentions"
      predicted mystical-type experiences. Limitations: non-controlled, self-selected, about 74% male, attrition, not
      ayahuasca-specific, no data on integration.
