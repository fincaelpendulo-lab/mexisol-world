Dear family,

Some of you have shared sacred ceremonies with us. Others have experienced the magic of Finca El Péndulo: the music, the laughter, the connections and the unforgettable moments that bring us together. Many of you first sat with us in the Osho Hut during the years of MexiSoul Retreats.

For all of those shared experiences, we are deeply grateful.

Today, we are excited to introduce you to **MexiSol World**.

A new and independent project that brings together Humberto Alcalá, Krista, Marina and our beautiful team at Finca El Péndulo.

Our hearts remain rooted in Mexico, but our vision reaches far beyond. MexiSol World brings together sacred retreats, plant medicine ceremonies, international journeys, music, festivals and gatherings that celebrate life, connection and transformation.

And we would love for you to be part of this new adventure.
