Finca El Péndulo sits on 14 acres in the highlands of Jalisco, outside Tepatitlán de Morelos. It is about an hour east of Guadalajara International Airport (GDL). Here is how to get here.

## Fly into Guadalajara (GDL)

Guadalajara's Miguel Hidalgo y Costilla International Airport has direct flights from many cities in Mexico, the United States and Canada. Flights are not included in the retreat price, so book a flight that lands on your arrival day with time to reach the Finca before dinner.

## Option 1: our airport pickup

The simplest way. A member of our staff meets you at the airport and drives you to the Finca.

- **$60 USD** one way
- **$110 USD** round trip

You get the driver's name and photo before you fly. Let us know your flight details once you book.

## Option 2: by bus

If you like to travel on your own:

1. Take a taxi from the airport to the **Nueva Central Camionera**, Guadalajara's main bus station.
2. Catch a bus to **Tepatitlán**. They leave about every 30 minutes.
3. Take a short taxi from Tepatitlán to the Finca.

It takes about two hours in total and costs under $30 USD.

## Option 3: by car

Search "Finca El Péndulo" on [Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/icPCz4ojjqUjLsNR6). The route uses the toll road most of the way.

Address: Camino al Saucillo 861, Tepatitlán de Morelos, Jalisco.

## What to expect on arrival

Arrival day is quiet. You check in, walk the land and meet the group over dinner. The first ceremony is on the second day. If you are arriving from far away, it can help to land a day early and rest in Guadalajara.

## Before you travel

- Pack layers: days are warm and nights are cool.
- Bring cash in USD or MXN for extras.
- Towels and linens are provided.
