You are curious. You have read about it. Now you are wondering whether ayahuasca is actually for you.

It is not a yes-or-no question. It is about timing, readiness and an honest look at yourself. Ayahuasca is not a magic cure. It is a tool, and like any tool it works best in certain hands, at certain moments.

## Who tends to benefit most

**People who have hit a wall.** They have tried therapy, books, coaching, sometimes medication, and something still feels unfinished. They are not in crisis. They function. But they are stuck in a pattern.

**People who are spiritually curious.** Not necessarily religious. Open to the idea that there is more to consciousness than the rational mind.

**People who want change, not just relief.** They are willing to do the integration work after the ceremony. The medicine shows you the door. You still have to walk through it.

**People who can sit with discomfort.** Nausea, purging and hard emotions are part of it. If the idea makes you want to run, it may not be the moment, or [psilocybin](ayahuasca-or-psilocybin) may be a better first step.

## Who should wait

**If you are in an acute mental health crisis.** Active thoughts of suicide, psychosis, or depression or anxiety that keeps you from functioning. Ayahuasca can destabilize a system that is already fragile. Stabilize first.

**If you take a medication that cannot be combined with it** and changing it, with your doctor, is not safe or possible for you. See [medications that are unsafe with ayahuasca](medications-unsafe-with-ayahuasca).

**If you want someone else to fix you.** The medicine does not fix. It shows. The work is yours.

**If you have no room for integration.** The retreat lasts seven days. Integration takes months.

**If you have already decided it won't work.** Healthy skepticism is welcome, and many of our guests arrive skeptical. A closed mind makes the experience harder.

## Questions to sit with

**Am I willing to go inward?** Ayahuasca does not take you to an outside paradise. It takes you into your history, your wounds and your beliefs about yourself.

**Have I really tried the other paths?** Some people get great results from therapy, meditation or medication. Have you given them a real chance, or are you impatient with them?

**Can I integrate this?** Will you give your insights time, or forget them the moment life gets busy?

**Am I doing this for the right reasons?** Chasing an experience, trying to impress someone, running from something? Or wanting to understand yourself?

**Do I have the time and the means?** A week away, plus a few months of attention afterward.

**Am I stable enough?** Not happy, not perfect. Stable. Ayahuasca amplifies what is already there.

**Do I actually want to change?** The medicine often shows what needs to change. Changing is uncomfortable.

## You may be ready if

- You have done real personal work and still feel stuck
- You are willing to adjust medications safely, with professional help
- You have room for integration in the months after
- You are curious, not desperate
- You see this as a tool, not a miracle
- You are open to change, even when it is hard

## What ayahuasca is and isn't

**It isn't** a magic cure, a substitute for therapy, a vacation, recreational, for everyone, or a one-time fix.

**It is** a mirror, a catalyst for change, a chance at deep self-knowledge, demanding, and something that asks for real integration.
