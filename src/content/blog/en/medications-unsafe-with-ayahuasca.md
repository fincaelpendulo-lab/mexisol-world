If you take any medication, you need to know what is safe and what is not. This is not something to guess about: the wrong combination can cause a serious medical emergency.

Below is a reference list. If you take anything on it, please don't skip ahead. Read it, then talk to us before you book.

**Never stop or change a prescribed medication on your own.** Any change has to be planned with the doctor who prescribed it.

This list also applies to **Satori**, the third ceremony in both our ayahuasca and psilocybin retreats, because it also contains an MAO inhibitor. More in [what is Satori](what-is-satori).

## Why ayahuasca interacts with so many medicines

Ayahuasca contains MAO inhibitors, which stop the body from breaking down serotonin and other neurotransmitters. Add a medicine that raises serotonin, and it can build up to dangerous levels. This is called **serotonin syndrome**: fast heart rate, high blood pressure, tremor, muscle rigidity, fever and confusion, and in severe cases seizures. Other combinations can cause a dangerous spike in blood pressure.

## Do not combine

### Antidepressants: SSRIs

Sertraline (Zoloft), escitalopram (Lexapro), paroxetine (Paxil), fluoxetine (Prozac), citalopram (Celexa), fluvoxamine (Luvox).

They raise serotonin. Together with an MAO inhibitor, the risk of serotonin syndrome is high. If you and your doctor decide to stop one, it has to be tapered, never stopped abruptly, and you need a clear gap afterward. Fluoxetine stays in the body much longer than the others, so its gap is longer.

### Antidepressants: SNRIs

Venlafaxine (Effexor), duloxetine (Cymbalta), desvenlafaxine (Pristiq). Same risk as SSRIs, plus effects on heart rhythm and blood pressure.

### Tricyclic antidepressants

Amitriptyline, nortriptyline, imipramine, doxepin, clomipramine. Risk of serotonin syndrome and of heart rhythm problems.

### MAOI antidepressants

Phenelzine (Nardil), tranylcypromine (Parnate), isocarboxazid (Marplan), selegiline. This doubles the same mechanism: serious risk of a hypertensive crisis and of serotonin syndrome.

### Tramadol and some other opioids

Tramadol, meperidine, methadone and tapentadol raise serotonin. With ayahuasca they carry a risk of serotonin syndrome and seizures.

### St. John's wort and other serotonergic supplements

St. John's wort, 5-HTP, SAMe and tryptophan behave like antidepressants in this context.

## High risk: talk to us first

### Stimulants

Methylphenidate (Ritalin, Concerta), amphetamines (Adderall, Vyvanse), phentermine and other weight-loss drugs. They raise heart rate and blood pressure, and the combination can be dangerous.

### Some blood pressure medicines

Methyldopa and reserpine in particular. They may need to be changed with your cardiologist's help.

### Linezolid

An antibiotic with MAO-inhibiting effects. Wait until the infection is treated and the course is finished.

### Migraine medicines

Triptans (sumatriptan and others) act on serotonin.

### Cold and cough medicines

Decongestants with pseudoephedrine or phenylephrine can raise blood pressure. Cough syrups with dextromethorphan (DXM) are serotonergic. Some antihistamines, such as chlorpheniramine, are too.

### Cancer treatment

Some chemotherapy and targeted drugs interact with MAO inhibitors. Chemotherapy or radiation in the past four weeks is a reason to wait.

## Needs individual review

- Lithium and other mood stabilizers
- Antipsychotics
- Benzodiazepines and sleeping pills
- Diabetes medication, because of fasting on ceremony days
- Blood thinners, thyroid medication and others

Tell us about all of them, including over-the-counter medicines, supplements and herbs such as kava, kratom, ginseng, rhodiola, ephedra or yohimbe.

## Usually fine

Paracetamol (acetaminophen) and ibuprofen in normal doses, basic vitamins and minerals. Still, list them.
